**Full description**

```
Find a line of Gurbani and turn it into a beautiful phone wallpaper or a WhatsApp and Instagram status image.

HOW IT WORKS
• Search by the first letter of each word, or by whole words, in English letters or Gurmukhi
• Or tap Random shabad
• Tap a line, choose a calm background and a Gurmukhi font, and save it to your gallery

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• All Gurbani comes live from BaniDB, the open Gurbani database by Khalis Foundation, and is shown exactly as it is received
• The Gurbani text can never be edited. You only choose the background, font, size and position
• Calm gradient backgrounds only, with no pictures of people
• Ads are family-safe and never appear on your wallpaper

MAKE IT YOURS
• 7 backgrounds: Amrit vela, Sarovar, Kesri, Marble, Gold leaf, Neela and Night
• 5 Gurmukhi fonts: Classic, Clean, Soft, Rounded and Modern
• Text size from 70% to 140%
• Place the line at the top (below your lock-screen clock), in the middle or at the bottom
• Show the pronunciation and the English meaning if you like
• Every wallpaper shows its reference, such as the Ang number

HIGH QUALITY
Wallpapers are saved at 1440 × 3120 pixels, sharp on almost every phone. Set one as your wallpaper from your phone's settings, or share it straight to WhatsApp, Instagram and more.

The app is free. A small banner ad and one short optional video, which unlocks saving and sharing for each wallpaper, keep it running.

Gurbani text, transliteration and translations come from BaniDB by Khalis Foundation. This app is not made by or affiliated with Khalis Foundation.
```
