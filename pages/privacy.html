Privacy Policy

Gurbani Wallpapers · Last updated 23 October 2026

Gurbani Wallpapers lets you find a line of Gurbani and turn it into a phone wallpaper or status image. This page explains what information is used when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Wallpapers you create are made on your device and are only shared where you choose to share or save them. The Android app asks for permission to save images to your gallery only when you tap "Save to gallery".

Gurbani search (BaniDB)

When you search or ask for a random shabad, your search text is sent to the BaniDB API run by Khalis Foundation so the matching lines can be shown. Your internet address is visible to their servers as part of any normal web request.

Fonts

Fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts. Google receives standard request information such as your internet address.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, internet address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the info (i) button on the search screen, under "Ad privacy settings". On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

The app may offer a short rewarded video ad that you can choose to watch to unlock saving and sharing a wallpaper.

Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

The web version of the app does not show ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
